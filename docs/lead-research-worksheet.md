# Lead research worksheet

Use this checklist for every business before it enters your pipeline. The goal
is simple: **every field can be traced to a public source, and every claimed
problem was seen with your own eyes.**

Copy [leads/leads.csv](../leads/leads.csv) to a working file (e.g.
`leads/private-2026-11.csv`, already ignored by git) or add the record directly
in Studio → Leads.

## Record fields

| Field | What to write | Example (fictional) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Business | Name exactly as published | Sample Bakery |
| Country | Main market | Canada |
| Category | From the Studio list | Small business |
| Website | Official URL, or “none” | none — Instagram only |
| Email | Public business email only | hello@samplebakery.example |
| Phone | Public business phone | +1 555 0100 |
| Contact person | Only if publicly named | (blank) |
| Status | New / Contacted / … | New |
| Follow-up | Date you will follow up | 2026-11-24 |
| Problems | Observed, specific, verifiable | Instagram bio has no hours; no menu; no way to order |
| Improvements | What you would change first | One-page site: hours, menu, location, order link |
| Opportunity | The service you would propose | Landing page + Google Business Profile link |
| Notes | Context, never sensitive data | Posts 3×/week; replies to DMs |
| Source URL | Where you verified the facts | https://maps.example/listing/… |
| Last verified | Date | 2026-11-19 |

> The example above is fictional and uses reserved example domains. Do not copy
> it into your pipeline as a real lead.

---

## Verification checklist

Before scoring, confirm:

- [ ] The business is currently operating (recent posts, reviews, hours).
- [ ] The website link is the official one — not a directory or an old domain.
- [ ] You opened the site on a phone-sized screen, not only desktop.
- [ ] You checked the menu, services, contact and booking paths yourself.
- [ ] The email/phone appears on a page the business controls.
- [ ] You saved the source URL and today's date.
- [ ] Your observation is a fact, not an insult or a guess.
- [ ] No personal data beyond a published business contact was collected.
- [ ] The business is not a recent, well-executed redesign.
- [ ] You have not contacted them before (check your own history).

## Scoring

| Signal | Points |
| --- | --- |
| No official website | +2 |
| Broken/unusable on mobile | +2 |
| Outdated design or stale content | +1 |
| Missing hours/location/contact/services | +1 |
| Insecure or very slow | +1 |
| No clear call to action | +1 |
| Actively advertising or promoting | +1 |
| Active business (recent activity) | +1 |

**3+** → worth a personalized email. **5+** → prioritize. **0–2** → archive, do
not contact.

## The observation sentence

This is the most important field. It becomes the first line of your email and
proves you actually looked.

Good:

- “The class timetable is a photo of a printed sheet, so it cannot be read on a
  phone without zooming.”
- “The site's last news post is from 2021 and the copyright still says 2020.”
- “There is no mention of opening hours anywhere on the homepage; they only
  appear in a Facebook post from March.”

Bad:

- “Your website is outdated.”
- “You need a modern website.”
- “I noticed you don't have a website” *(when they do)*.

If you cannot write a specific sentence, the lead is not ready.

## Turning a row into a Studio lead

1. Open Studio → Leads → **Add lead**.
2. Paste business, country, category, website, email, phone.
3. Paste the observation into **Website problems** and the proposed fix into
   **Suggested improvements**.
4. Set the opportunity and status.
5. Save. Use **Draft email** from the edit dialog to jump to Outreach.

Studio stores data in this browser only. Export a JSON backup after each
session. CSV export is for spreadsheets; JSON is for restore.

## Privacy and ethics reminders

- Research only what the business publishes for customers.
- Do not join private groups to harvest contacts.
- Do not use personal social profiles or guessed emails.
- Delete a lead and its notes if the business asks you to stop.
- Never record health, financial or identity information.
- If in doubt, leave it out.
