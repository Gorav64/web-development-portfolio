# Proposal templates

Studio → Proposals generates a printable estimate from a short form. This page
explains every block so you can rewrite it in your own voice and adapt it to a
specific client.

**A proposal is not a contract.** Do not describe it as signed, accepted or
binding until both sides have confirmed the final scope in writing. Never
accept a project or agree to payment terms on a client's behalf.

---

## Structure

1. Header — your name, email, portfolio
2. Prepared for / date / validity
3. Project type and estimated timeline
4. Overview
5. Scope of work
6. Not included
7. Investment (estimate table)
8. Payment milestones
9. Timeline and dependencies
10. Revisions
11. Delivery and handover
12. Next steps
13. Estimate disclaimer

---

## Header and metadata

```text
Website proposal
Prepared for [Client / Business]

[Your name]
[Your email]
[Portfolio URL]

Date: [today]
Valid until: [today + 14 days]
Project type: [Business website]
Estimated timeline: [N] calendar days
```

---

## Overview

Two or three sentences. Use the client's own goal.

> [Client] needs a [project type] that is clear for visitors, fast on a phone
> and easy to maintain. This proposal covers design adaptation, responsive
> development, testing and handover.

Optional context paragraph (from Studio's notes field):

> The site's main job is to [primary conversion goal]. The current site's
> biggest problem is [specific issue observed], and this scope addresses it
> first.

Avoid: “This will transform your business”, “guaranteed to increase sales”.

## Scope of work

### Variant A — Landing page (1 page)

- One responsive landing page designed around a single action
- Hero, benefits, proof, offer, FAQ, contact/booking section as agreed
- Semantic HTML, modern CSS, minimal JavaScript
- Basic on-page SEO: title, description, headings, social preview
- Cross-browser checks and testing at phone, tablet and desktop sizes
- Source files and a short handover note

### Variant B — Multi-page business site (3–5 pages)

- [N] responsive pages: Home, About, Services, Contact, plus [page]
- Shared header, navigation and footer
- Mobile menu and accessible focus states
- Contact form (mailto fallback or form service configured by the client)
- Basic on-page SEO on every page
- Cross-browser checks and testing at phone, tablet and desktop sizes
- Source files and a short handover note

Add agreed add-ons as explicit bullets: booking embed, second language,
analytics, speed pass, copy polishing, animations, API integration.

---

## Not included

Always list exclusions. At minimum:

- Domain registration, hosting fees and third-party paid services
- Content writing beyond light polishing of supplied copy
- Logo, brand identity, photography or paid stock
- Ongoing maintenance, retainers or emergency support
- New features outside the listed scope
- Taxes, bank charges and payment-processing fees

## Investment

Start from the beginner tiers:

| Service | INR | Approx. USD |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Basic landing page | ₹3,000 | $32 |
| Small business website | ₹4,000 | $43 |
| Simple website redesign | ₹3,000–₹4,000 | $32–$43 |

These are starting suggestions, not fixed prices. Show every proposal in **INR and
USD**, using a dated conversion rate (₹93 per USD is the current planning rate). USD
is indicative; agree the invoice currency before work starts.

```text
| Item                              | INR    | Approx. USD |
| --------------------------------- | -----: | ----------: |
| [Project type] — [N] pages        | ₹X,XXX |          $XX |
| Additional pages ([N])            |   ₹500 |           $5 |
| [Add-on]                          |   ₹XXX |           $XX |
| Priority delivery (25%)           |   ₹XXX |           $XX |
| Total                             | ₹X,XXX |          $XX |
```

Follow with exactly what it is:

> This is an estimate based on the information available today. The final quote
> is confirmed after the discovery call and content review.

Do not present it as a fixed price unless you are prepared to absorb all
unknowns. Nothing is sent to a client until Vishal approves the figure.

## Payment milestones

**Projects below ₹25,000**

| Milestone | Share | Amount |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Deposit — project start | 50% | ₹X / $X |
| Final delivery | 50% | ₹X / $X |

**Projects at or above ₹25,000**

| Milestone | Share | Amount |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Deposit — project start | 40% | ₹X / $X |
| Design & development milestone | 40% | ₹X / $X |
| Final delivery | 20% | ₹X / $X |

Add:

> Work begins after the deposit is received. The site is handed over after the
> final payment clears. Payment details are shared separately through an agreed
> invoicing method — never by emailing card or bank credentials.

---

## Timeline and dependencies

```text
Estimated [N] calendar days from the date content and access are provided.
Delays in feedback, content or third-party approvals will shift the delivery
date accordingly.
```

Be specific about what you need and when:

- Copy for each section
- Logo files and images
- Hosting/domain access (client-owned)
- One decision-maker for consolidated feedback

---

## Revisions

```text
Two rounds of revisions are included during design and build. Additional rounds
are ₹500 (about $5) each. Feedback is collected in one consolidated list per
round. A change of direction after approval may be treated as a new scope.
```

---

## Delivery and handover

```text
Delivery includes the final static files, a short README explaining structure
and editing, and one walkthrough call. Deployment is [performed by me /
documented for your team] on infrastructure owned by you.

Files are delivered after the final milestone is settled. Ongoing updates,
hosting management and new features are not included unless agreed separately.
```

---

## Next steps

1. Confirm the scope and answer any open questions.
2. Confirm the final quote in writing.
3. Pay the first milestone.
4. Share content, brand assets and access details.
5. Kick-off call and first design direction.

---

## Estimate disclaimer

Put this at the bottom of every proposal:

> Prices are estimates, not a binding contract until both parties confirm the
> final scope in writing. No results, rankings, traffic or revenue are
> guaranteed. Domain, hosting and third-party costs are paid by the client.
> USD figures are indicative at the stated conversion rate; the agreed invoice
> currency governs payment.

---

## Tone checklist

- [ ] Written in plain language, no agency jargon.
- [ ] Specific to this client — not reusable with only the name changed.
- [ ] Every promise is deliverable by you.
- [ ] No fake testimonials, client logos or case studies.
- [ ] No guaranteed traffic, ranking or revenue.
- [ ] Exclusions are explicit.
- [ ] INR and USD are both shown, with the conversion rate and date.
- [ ] Validity date is stated.
- [ ] A human has read it end to end before sending.
- [ ] Vishal has approved the final figure; nothing was sent automatically.

## How Studio generates it

| Studio field | Appears as |
| --- | --- |
| Client name | Header and overview |
| Project type | Project type, base price, timeline |
| Pages | Page count line and extra-page price |
| Add-ons | Scope bullets and investment rows |
| Priority delivery | +25% line and shorter timeline |
| Currency | Amounts displayed in the chosen currency, with INR and USD both shown |
| Notes | Extra overview paragraph |
| Settings → name/email/portfolio | Header and footer |

The generated paper is a draft. Edit the result in your own document if the
project needs custom terms, then send it through your normal channel. Studio
does not send or sign proposals.
