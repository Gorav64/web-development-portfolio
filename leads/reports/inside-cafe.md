## Why it matters

The content below the hero is solid; the first screen is the weak point, and it is the part every visitor sees.

## Suggested first scope

Rebuild the hero with the cafe name, offer, hours and location, add a visible menu CTA, fix the page title and keep the existing booking and order links.

This is a starting point, not a quote. It excludes domain and hosting fees, paid plugins or fonts, stock photography, brand identity, ongoing maintenance and taxes. Confirm scope after a discovery call.
