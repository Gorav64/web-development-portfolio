05 Work process

A clear path from first call to launch.

Six steps, no surprises. You always know what is happening, what is next, and what I need from you.

  1. 01

    Discovery

    A short call or written brief to understand your business, audience and the one thing the website must achieve.

    Output: goals & scope notes
  2. 02

    Planning

    Page list, content outline, sitemap and timeline. We agree on exactly what is being built before any design starts.

    Output: sitemap & timeline
  3. 03

    Design

    Layout direction and visual style for the key pages. You review and approve the look before development begins.

    Output: design sign-off
  4. 04

    Development

    Responsive build with clean HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Progress previews shared at natural checkpoints.

    Output: preview build
  5. 05

    Testing

    Checks across phone, tablet and desktop sizes, plus links, forms, loading behaviour and basic accessibility.

    Output: test report
  6. 06

    Delivery

    Files, deployment, a short handover document and a walkthrough so you can make simple edits yourself.

    Output: live site & handover
